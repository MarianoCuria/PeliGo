# PeliGo — Investor Pitch

---

## "Netflix, Amazon, Disney+, HBO, Paramount, Apple TV+... which one is it on?"

That's a question **millions of Argentines ask every week**. And the answer today requires opening 6 apps, searching in each one, and praying that it's on the one you pay for.

**PeliGo solves that in 5 seconds.**

---

## The Problem

### Streaming fragmentation is the new "500 channels and nothing to watch"

In Argentina there are **more than 10 active streaming platforms**. The average user pays for 2-3 services and has no quick way to know where the content they want to watch is.

**The pain is real and everyday:**
- "Is Breaking Bad on Netflix or on Amazon?" → I opened both. It's on neither. It was on HBO.
- "Where do I watch Oppenheimer?" → I search for it in 4 apps. I find it to rent on Apple TV for $2,500.
- "When does Dune 3 come out on streaming?" → There's no easy way to know.

**Key data point:** 73% of streaming users in LATAM have difficulty finding specific content across their platforms (Statista 2025).

**Average time lost:** 15-20 minutes searching for where to watch something. That time should be 5 seconds.

---

## The Solution

### PeliGo: Search, find, watch.

An ultra-fast **Progressive Web App** that tells you in seconds where to watch any movie or series in Argentina.

**User flow:**
1. Open PeliGo (or search Google for "dónde ver Breaking Bad").
2. Type the title.
3. In 2 seconds you see: Netflix (stream included), Amazon (rent $1,200), Apple TV (buy $2,500).
4. Tap "Ver ahora" (Watch now) → It takes you straight to the platform.

**Key differentiators:**
- **Localized for Argentina** (platforms, prices in ARS, local trending).
- **Smart alerts** ("Notify me when Dune 3 is on Netflix").
- **Installable PWA** — no App Store, loads in 1 second.
- **Obsessively simple UX** — zero friction, immediate response.

## Business Model

### Phase 1: Freemium (Year 1)

| Tier       | Price        | Includes                                                     |
|------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Free**   | $0          | Unlimited search, trending, 3 alerts, basic favorites       |
| **Pro**    | USD $1.99/mo | Unlimited alerts, advanced filters, no ads, export lists    |

**Projection:**
- 50,000 MAU × 5% Pro conversion = 2,500 subscribers
- Revenue: 2,500 × $1.99 = **$4,975/mo** (~$60K/year)

### Phase 2: Affiliates (Year 1-2)

Every time a user taps "Ver ahora" (Watch now) and rents/buys on a platform, PeliGo earns an affiliate commission.

- Amazon Associates: 2-4% commission.
- Apple TV Affiliate: up to 7%.
- Potential revenue: $0.10-0.50 per transactional click.
- At 100K MAU with 10% click-through: **$10K-50K/year additional.**

### Phase 3: Partnerships & Ads (Year 2+)

- **Sponsored placements:** Platforms pay for a featured position in results.
- **Contextual display ads:** Non-intrusive, only if necessary.
- **Data insights:** Anonymized trend reports for platforms/studios.
- **B2B API:** Media and blogs embed a "Dónde verlo" (Where to watch it) widget (revenue share).

1. **Fragmentation at its peak.** There have never been so many platforms competing in Argentina. User pain is the highest in history.

4. **Historically low development cost.** With modern tools (Next.js, NestJS, third-party APIs), a team of 1-2 people can launch a quality product.

5. **Argentina as a springboard.** You validate in AR, then scale to 20 Spanish-speaking countries with the same product.
