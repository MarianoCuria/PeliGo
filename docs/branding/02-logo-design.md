# PeliGo — Logo Design

## Logo Philosophy

The PeliGo logo must be **instantly recognizable**, work at minimal sizes (PWA icon 192x192 and 512x512), and communicate three concepts: **cinema/streaming + search + speed**.

---

## Concept Proposals

### Proposal 1 — "Play Search" (Recommended)

**Concept:** A play button (triangle) integrated inside a search magnifying glass.

**Description:**
- The magnifying glass represents search.
- The play triangle inside the glass circle represents streaming/content.
- The handle of the glass tilts like a forward arrow, suggesting speed ("Go").

**Application:**
- Icon only: The glass-play works perfectly at 32x32px.
- With text: "Peli" in regular weight + "Go" in bold, both in Space Grotesk.
- The play triangle uses a red→purple gradient.

**Colors:** Gradient `#E63946` → `#6C2BD9` on the play. Glass in white (dark) or black (light).

---

### Proposal 2 — "Film Reel Arrow"

**Concept:** A simplified film reel with a built-in arrow/direction.

**Description:**
- Minimalist silhouette of a film frame (two perforations on each side).
- Inside the frame, an arrow pointing right ("Go").
- Ultra simplified to work as a favicon.

**Application:**
- Icon only: Frame + arrow.
- With text: The frame as the "O" in "PeliGo", with the arrow exiting the frame.

**Colors:** Frame in `#E63946`, arrow in `#6C2BD9`.

---

### Proposal 3 — "Speed Cursor"

**Concept:** A cursor/pointer shaped like a play triangle with speed lines.

**Description:**
- A stylized digital cursor that is also a play button.
- Two motion lines behind it, suggesting speed.
- Conveys: digital + play + speed.

**Application:**
- Icon only: Cursor-play with speed lines.
- With text: The cursor replaces the dot of the "i" in "PeliGo".

**Colors:** Cursor in the primary gradient. Speed lines in `#6C2BD9` with opacity.

---

### Proposal 4 — "P-Play"

**Concept:** The letter "P" stylized with a negative-space play button.

**Description:**
- The letter P of PeliGo in Space Grotesk Bold.
- The interior space of the P is cut out as a play triangle.
- Minimalist and very recognizable.

**Application:**
- Icon only: The P with a negative-space play, on a dark background.
- With text: Stylized P + "eliGo" in Space Grotesk regular.
- The "G" is capitalized to emphasize "Go".

**Colors:** P in `#E63946`, the play fill in the background color (negative space).

---

### Proposal 5 — "Go Button"

**Concept:** A circular button with a play, like a premium streaming button.

**Description:**
- Circle with a red→purple gradient border.
- Centered play triangle.
- A subtle glow effect around it.
- Directly evokes "hitting play" on something.

**Application:**
- Icon only: The play circle.
- With text: Button on the left + "PeliGo" on the right.
- In PWA: The button as the central splash screen.

**Colors:** Gradient border `#E63946` → `#6C2BD9`. Play in `#F0F0F5`. Glow in `rgba(230,57,70,0.3)`.

---

### Proposal 6 — "Binoculars Play"

**Concept:** Simplified binoculars where the lenses are play buttons.

**Description:**
- Two circles (binoculars) with play triangles inside.
- Concept: literally "looking for what to watch".
- Fun but professional.

**Application:**
- Icon only: Binoculars.
- With text: Binoculars as a separator between "Peli" and "Go".

**Colors:** Circles in `#1A1A2E`, plays in `#E63946`.

---

### Proposal 7 — "Popcorn Pin"

**Concept:** A location pin shaped like popcorn.

**Description:**
- A map pin (location) whose top is shaped like popcorn.
- Concept: "finding where" to watch something.
- Culturally Argentine (popcorn = cinema).

**Application:**
- Icon only: The popcorn-pin.
- With text: Pin before "PeliGo".

**Colors:** Pin in `#E63946`, popcorn in `#F59E0B`, shadow in `#6C2BD9`.

---

### Proposal 8 — "Slash Play"

**Concept:** The "/" in PeliGo stylized as a rotated play button.

**Description:**
- "Peli/Go" where the slash is a play triangle rotated 90°.
- Ultra minimalist, typographic.
- Works as a pure wordmark.

**Application:**
- Icon only: Just the slash-play in a square.
- With text: "Peli▶Go" in Space Grotesk.

**Colors:** Text in `#F0F0F5`, slash-play in `#E63946`.

## Logo Versions

### For each final proposal, generate:

| Version                    | Specification                           |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **App icon (PWA)**        | 512x512px, 20% safe-area padding        |
| **Favicon**               | 32x32px, 16x16px                        |
| **Horizontal logo**       | Icon + wordmark, 40px height            |
| **Vertical logo**         | Icon on top + wordmark below            |
| **Icon only**             | No text, square                         |
| **Dark mode**             | On `#0D0D0D` background                  |
| **Light mode**            | On `#FAFAFA` background                  |
| **White monochrome**      | For use over photos/gradients           |
| **Black monochrome**      | For print and documents                 |

### Exclusion Zone
- Minimum space around the logo: equivalent to the height of the "G" in PeliGo.
- Never place elements within this zone.

### Incorrect Uses
- Do not stretch or distort.
- Do not change the gradient colors.
- Do not add drop shadows to the logo.
- Do not rotate the logo.
- Do not use over backgrounds that compete in color (similar red or purple).

## Proposed Slogans

| Slogan                                | Tone                    |
|---------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **"Encontrá dónde verlo"** (Find where to watch it) | Direct, Argentine       |
| **"Tu peli, al toque"** (Your movie, right away)    | Casual, fast            |
| **"Buscá. Encontrá. Mirá."** (Search. Find. Watch.) | Imperative, clear       |
| **"¿Dónde lo paso? PeliGo."** (Where do I play it? PeliGo.) | Question/answer  |
| **"Todo el streaming, un solo lugar"** (All of streaming, one place) | Descriptive |
| **"Dale play, sabé dónde"** (Hit play, know where)  | Young, direct           |

**Recommendation:** "Encontrá dónde verlo" as the main slogan — it's clear, uses Argentine voseo, and communicates the exact value of the app.

---

## Final Recommendation

**Proposal 1 ("Play Search")** is the strongest because:
1. It communicates the two core values (search + watch) in a single icon.
2. It works at all sizes.
3. It is simple and memorable.
4. The red→purple gradient gives it a unique identity.
5. It differentiates from any existing streaming platform.

**Proposal 4 ("P-Play")** as a strong alternative for its extreme minimalism and typographic strength.
